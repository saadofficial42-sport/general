How a placement works

Eight steps from demand letter to arrival.

This is the legal route every overseas job from Pakistan follows. We manage each step and keep both the employer and the candidate informed.

  1. 01
    Employer

    Demand & power of attorney

    The employer issues a demand letter and power of attorney, attested by the Pakistani mission in their country.

  2. 02
    BE&OE

    Permission & advertisement

    We obtain Bureau of Emigration permission and publish the approved job advertisement.

  3. 03
    Selection

    Shortlist, interview, trade test

    Candidates are screened, interviewed and tested, in person or online, with the employer.

  4. 04
    Health

    Medical examination

    Selected workers complete medicals at GCC-approved or embassy-approved centres.

  5. 05
    Embassy

    Visa processing

    Work visas and e-visas are processed and stamped through the relevant embassy.

  6. 06
    Protectorate

    Protector registration

    The Foreign Service Agreement is registered with the Protector of Emigrants, with insurance and OPF fees paid.

  7. 07
    Departure

    Briefing & ticketing

    Pre-departure orientation on contract rights, culture and safety, then flights are booked.

  8. 08
    Aftercare

    Arrival & follow-up

    We confirm arrival with the employer and stay in contact through the first months of the contract.

Protect yourself

How to spot a genuine overseas job

  • Check the promoter's licence on the BE&OE list of active OEPs.
  • Make sure your contract is registered with the Protector of Emigrants before you fly.
  • Pay only the charges allowed under the Emigration Rules, and only against an official receipt.
  • Never travel on a visit visa for a job. It is illegal and leaves you unprotected.
Questions

Frequently asked.

How long does it take to go abroad after selection?

For most Gulf countries it takes 4 to 10 weeks from selection to departure. The time depends on the medical, visa processing at the embassy and Protector registration. European work permits usually take longer, often 3 to 6 months.

What documents do I need to apply?

A valid passport (at least 6 months' validity, ideally 2 years), CNIC, recent photographs, educational and experience certificates, and a driving licence for driving jobs. Professionals may need attested degrees.

Do I have to pay any fees?

Only the charges permitted by the Government of Pakistan under the Emigration Rules, plus official costs such as medical, Protector, insurance and OPF fees. Everything is paid against an official receipt. Many employers cover visa and ticket costs.

I'm an employer abroad. How do I start?

Send us your requirement: trades, numbers, salary, contract length and benefits. We'll advise on the documents needed (demand letter and power of attorney) and share a recruitment timeline and quotation.

Can you arrange trade tests with our engineers present?

Yes. Your team can witness tests in person in Rawalpindi or Islamabad, or we can run them at partner centres and share recorded video and scores.

Where is your office?

We are in Rawalpindi and serve the whole Islamabad–Rawalpindi region. Walk-in visitors are welcome during office hours. See the contact details below.
