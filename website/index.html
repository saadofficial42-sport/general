How we recruit

From your requirement to workers on site in six steps.

A clear, repeatable process, whether you need five drivers or five hundred construction workers.

  1. 01
    Brief

    Requirement & job specs

    You share trades, numbers, salary, benefits and timelines. We confirm specs, skill level and documents needed.

  2. 02
    Source

    Sourcing & advertising

    We draw on our candidate database, referral network, vocational institutes and targeted job campaigns across Pakistan.

  3. 03
    Screen

    Screening & trade testing

    CV checks, interviews, practical trade tests with video, document and experience verification.

  4. 04
    Select

    Client selection & offers

    You interview shortlisted candidates in person or online. Selected workers receive written offers.

  5. 05
    Prepare

    Medicals & documentation

    Medical fitness, visas and work permits, contracts and pre-departure orientation, coordinated end to end.

  6. 06
    Deliver

    Deployment & aftercare

    Travel, arrival handover and follow-up during the contract, with replacements handled as agreed.
