Partnership models

Choose the model that fits how you hire.

Best for permanent roles

Direct placement

We source, screen and present candidates; you employ them from day one.

Best for projects & peaks

Contract staffing

Workers stay on our payroll while working at your site, for the length of your project.

Best for continuous hiring

Recruitment process outsourcing

We run your hiring end to end as an extension of your HR team, with agreed service levels.

Best for large mobilisations

Project & volume hiring

Dedicated teams, trade-test days and walk-in drives to fill hundreds of positions quickly.

Best for trying before hiring

Temp-to-permanent

Workers start on a trial contract and move to your payroll once both sides are happy (Pakistan).
