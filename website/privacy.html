Privacy policy

How RS Links Consultants handles your personal information.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

RS Links Consultants ("we", "us") is a recruitment and HR consultancy based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our recruitment services, why we collect it and how we protect it.

Information we collect

  • Job seekers: name, contact details, CNIC and passport details, CV, education, trade certificates, work history, references, photographs and, where a role requires it, medical fitness information.
  • Employers and partners: contact names, business contact details, company information and hiring requirements.
  • Website visitors: information you enter in our forms, and basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To match candidates with suitable vacancies and present them to employers.
  • To arrange interviews, trade tests, medicals, visas and travel where applicable.
  • To respond to enquiries and manage our relationships with clients and partners.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

Sharing your information

We share candidate information only with prospective employers, and with the government bodies, embassies, medical centres and travel providers needed to complete a placement. We do not sell personal information.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Access is limited to staff who need it for their work.

How long we keep it

We keep candidate profiles for as long as they are useful for future vacancies, unless you ask us to delete them. Records we must keep by law are retained for the required period.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, at any time. Contact us using the details below.

Messaging services

If you contact us through WhatsApp or another third-party service, that service's own privacy policy also applies.
