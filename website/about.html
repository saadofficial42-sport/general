RS Links Consultants is a private limited recruitment and HR consultancy based in Rawalpindi, serving the twin cities and employers across Pakistan and abroad.

Who we are

A recruitment firm for the hands-on workforce.

Every building, factory line, hospital ward and delivery fleet runs on skilled, dependable people. Too often, employers struggle to find them and good workers struggle to find honest opportunities.

We started RS Links Consultants to close that gap. From our office in Rawalpindi, next to the capital, we recruit blue-collar and technical workers from every province of Pakistan and match them with employers who value their skills.

That idea is in our name and our seal: two interlocking links, one for the worker and one for the employer, joined at the heart of a stamp that stands for trust and keeping our word.

Alongside recruitment, our consultancy team helps companies plan their workforce, outsource staffing and strengthen HR, so hiring is only one part of how we support our clients.

Ethical recruitment

Our commitments to workers.

We follow internationally recognised principles of fair recruitment.

  • Clear, written job offers in a language the worker understands.
  • Workers keep their own passports and documents.
  • No discrimination on religion, ethnicity or background.
  • Overseas deployments follow the legal emigration route required by the Government of Pakistan.
  • Personal data handled confidentially, as set out in our privacy policy.
