Residency by investment

Invest abroad and become a resident.

Several countries grant residency, and in some cases citizenship, to people who make a qualifying investment such as buying property, investing in a business or contributing to a government fund. We help you choose the right programme and manage the application from start to finish.

What we do for investors
  • Compare programmes by budget, timeline, family needs and travel benefits.
  • Eligibility check and a complete document plan for you and your family.
  • Coordination with property developers, lawyers and authorised agents in each country.
  • Application filing, follow-up and renewals.
Book an investor consultation

Programmes we advise on

Investment amounts, eligibility rules and processing times are set by each government and change from time to time. We confirm the current requirements in writing at your consultation. Approval rests with the relevant authorities.
