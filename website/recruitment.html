Skill levels

From certified tradespeople to general helpers.

Skilled

Certified, experienced tradespeople: welders, electricians, HVAC and instrument technicians, heavy-equipment operators, staff nurses, chefs.

Semi-skilled

Trained workers with hands-on experience: drivers, machine operators, caregivers, masons' assistants, cooks, forklift operators, security guards.

General / unskilled

Reliable workers for labour-intensive roles: helpers, loaders, packers, cleaners, farm and warehouse workers.
